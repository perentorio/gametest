Player versus computer. First to 7 wins.

Pong requires a browser with HTML Canvas support. Use W and S or the up and down arrow keys to move your paddle.

Select Start game to play.

How to play

Move with W / S or ↑ / ↓. During play, press Space to pause or resume while the court is focused.

Return the ball past the computer's paddle to score. The first player to reach 7 points wins.
